How Resume Prune works

The whole app in two minutes.

The big picture

Your resume is uploaded once. For every job after that, you paste the posting, Resume Prune checks the match, prunes your resume to fit, and writes the cover letter. Download both, send them, mark the tweak Applied.

One posting, start to finish, is one tweak. Sprint gives you 20 a week, Pro gives you 40 a month. Checking the match is what spends it; the resume and cover letter from that match are included.

Every tweak has a status

TweakedYou checked the match. Nothing sent yet.
AppliedYou sent it. Grows the vine under Your hunt.
InterviewingThey called. The vine gets a bud.
OfferThey want you. The vine gets fruit.
ClosedDone, either way. Drops off the vine, stays in history.

You set it with the dropdown under each past tweak. Nothing else moves it.

What each part is for

  • The form: role title, company, job description. Check the match spends one tweak.
  • Match analysis: score out of 10, strong alignments, gaps. A gap you've actually done? Tap I can fill this gap and every future check uses it.
  • Resume preview: pick Classic, Modern, or Executive, download the PDF, or publish it as a web page at /r/your-name.
  • What got pruned and why: every cut, with the reason. Read it before you send.
  • This period: your plan and tweaks left. Sprint resets weekly, Pro monthly.
  • Past tweaks: click one to bring it all back. The x deletes it for good.
  • Edit resume (top bar) replaces your base resume. Past tweaks keep theirs.

A normal day

  1. Paste a posting with its role title. Check the match.
  2. Fill any gap you've actually done. Prune the resume, generate the cover letter.
  3. Read what got pruned, download both PDFs, apply. Mark it Applied.

Just starting?

  1. Find your tweak count under This period. That's your budget.
  2. Paste one real posting and check the match. The role title is only a label.
  3. Prune it, read the pruning notes, download the Classic PDF.
