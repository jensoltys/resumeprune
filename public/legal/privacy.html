2. What we collect

2.1 Free sample (no account required)

  • Email address you submit on the Try it page so we can email you the rendered sample. We don't store the address itself. We store a one-way hash of it and the part after the @ (the email domain), which we use to enforce the free-sample limits.
  • Resume text you paste or upload, and the job description you paste, for the duration of one request.
  • Rendered output (the pruned resume + cover letter we generated), stored behind a private, unguessable link so you can revisit it. A daily cleanup job deletes the stored copy 30 days after it was made, and the link stops working then.
  • Hashed IP address and the date of each free sample, used for rate-limiting and abuse prevention.

2.2 Pro / Sprint account

  • Email address and a password hash (we use PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations; the plaintext password never touches our database).
  • Resume text you upload, and the structured version our system generates from it.
  • Tweak history: each job description you've worked on, its role and company, your match score and analysis, the application status you set, and the tailored resume + cover letter we produced.
  • Gap answers: experience you add to fill a gap, the gap it answers, and the job you say it happened at.
  • Published resume pages, if you publish one: the resume, its link name, style, color, headline and links, whether you let search engines list it, and a count of views. Anyone with the link can see the page while it's live. Your analysis and the job description are never shown there.
  • Usage counters: how many tweaks you've made this billing period.
  • Subscription metadata: your Stripe customer ID, subscription ID, status, and billing-event log. We do not store your card number, Stripe does that.
  • Profile fields you choose to set: target role, target industry.
  • Email-verification status and account-creation date.

2.3 Automatically collected

  • Cloudflare edge logs: request URL, timestamp, your IP, user agent, and response status. Held by Cloudflare under Cloudflare's privacy policy. We don't store these ourselves. They're operational.
  • Rate-limit records: to block abuse, we log each request to sensitive actions (like logging in, signing up, or generating a sample) with a timestamp and a key. For most actions the key is a one-way hash of your IP address, never the address itself. For some it's your account ID or a hashed email. A daily cleanup job deletes these records once they're more than 2 days old.
  • Sign-in security log: when someone logs in, fails a login, changes or resets a password, or deletes an account, our server writes one line with the type of event, the time, a one-way hash of the IP address, and either the account ID or a one-way hash of the email typed in. It never holds a password, a raw email, or a raw IP. These lines sit in Cloudflare's Workers Logs, which keeps them for up to 7 days.
  • Auth session cookie: one cookie named rp_session (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax). Holds a signed JWT. Used only to keep you logged in.
  • Feedback widget: if you're logged in and press Send on the in-app feedback widget, we receive your message, the type you picked (bug, idea, or other), the page URL you sent it from, your browser's user-agent string, and your email only if you typed it in.

6. How long we keep it

  • Free-sample rendered output: deleted by a daily cleanup job 30 days after it was made. If you create an account with the same email and later delete it, your free samples are deleted along with it.
  • Free-sample records (hashed email, email domain, hashed IP, date): kept so the free-sample limits (counted over 30 days) work, then deleted by the same daily job once they're 35 days old, or right away when you delete an account with that email.
  • Rate-limit records: deleted by a daily cleanup job once they're more than 2 days old.
  • Email verification codes and password-reset links: deleted when used, or by the daily cleanup job 7 days after they expire.
  • Sign-in security log: kept by Cloudflare Workers Logs for up to 7 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Account email, password hash, profile, stored resume, tweak history, gap fills, published resume pages, and payment events log: until you delete your account. When you do, they're deleted from our database right away.
  • Stripe's records: Stripe keeps its own record of your payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Email-opt-out records: kept indefinitely (in hashed form) so we honor your opt-out even if you sign up again later.
  • Cloudflare edge logs: held by Cloudflare under its own retention policy. We don't store our own copy.
  • Database restore history: our database host keeps an automatic restore history for up to 30 days (Cloudflare D1 Time Travel), so deleted data can stay in that history until it rolls off.

9. Cookies, browser storage, and Do Not Track

Resume Prune sets one cookie: rp_session. It holds your signed login JWT. It's HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax. It's strictly necessary to keep you logged in, so under EU ePrivacy rules it does not require consent.

Resume Prune also saves a few small items in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device, and they're needed for the app to work the way you'd expect:

  • rp_try_resume: on the free Try it page, a copy of the resume you pasted, so you don't have to paste it again next time. It clears itself after 30 days, and we never read it from there. The Forget it link under the resume box deletes it.
  • rp_aff_ref: the referral code from an affiliate link, if you arrived through one. It's sent with your checkout so the right affiliate gets credit. A companion key, rp_aff_ref_at, records when you clicked, and both are dropped 60 days after your last affiliate link click.
  • rp_pending_plan: the plan you picked (Sprint or Pro) before creating an account, so checkout can pick up where you left off after you sign up or log in. It's removed once you head to checkout.
  • rp_tour_done: remembers that you've finished or skipped the welcome tour, so it doesn't show again.
  • rp_pdf_…, rp_pub_…, rp_questdone_…, rp_questhide_…: small flags (ending in your account ID) that remember you've downloaded a PDF, published a resume link, finished the first-week checklist, or hidden it, so the dashboard doesn't celebrate the same thing twice.

We do not set marketing cookies. We do not use Google Analytics or third-party tracking pixels. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless. There is no consent banner because there are no non-essential cookies to consent to.

Do Not Track. Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. Resume Prune doesn't use advertising or tracking tools that follow you across other websites, so it works the same whether or not your browser sends that signal. We don't let any third party collect information about your activity on Resume Prune to track you across other sites over time.

11. How we protect your data

We follow standard security practices for a small SaaS:

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS with HSTS).
  • All data is encrypted at rest by Cloudflare (D1) and Stripe (payment data).
  • Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations, per-user salt). The plaintext is never stored or logged.
  • Sessions can be globally invalidated on demand (every JWT carries a token version we can bump).
  • We rate-limit all public endpoints and have automated rules for abusive traffic.
  • Secrets are stored in Cloudflare's secret store, never in source code.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a personal-data breach, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware (as GDPR Article 33 requires) and notify affected users without undue delay if the breach is likely to cause significant harm.
